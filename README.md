### The 3/8 Exponent for Shortest Directed Paths

In a KMSW cell encoded by n independent uniform steps from {(1, −1), (−1, 0), (0, 1)}, let Δ<sub>n</sub> be the largest finite shortest directed-path distance, measured in edges. Every median m<sub>n</sub> of Δ<sub>n</sub> satisfies m<sub>n</sub> = n<sup>3/8+o(1)</sup>.

### Graph Limits of Longest Directed Paths

For the uniform infinite bipolar-oriented triangulation, rescale north- and west-going exploration times by n and longest directed-path lengths by n<sup>3/4</sup>. The resulting fields are tight, and every subsequential closed-graph limit is almost surely a locally bounded continuous function on the limiting domain of directed-comparable edge pairs.

### Nontrivial Limits of Shortest Directed Paths

For the uniform infinite bipolar-oriented triangulation, rescale exploration times by n and shortest directed-path lengths by the upper integer median of the maximal finite distance in an n-step KMSW cell. Subsequential closed-graph limits are continuous functions. In limits taken jointly with the primal and dual peanosphere data, the function is almost surely not identically zero.

### Mesh-Only Convergence for Orthogonal Tilings

For orthogonal tilings in dimension d ≥ 2 with mesh ε → 0, discrete harmonic extensions of smooth harmonic boundary data converge uniformly without the original theorem’s additional geometric alternatives. For h harmonic and C<sup>2</sup> near a bounded domain compactly inside the tiled region, the supplement gives error ≤ C‖h‖<sub>C²</sub> ε<sup>4/(d+4)</sup>, hence exponent 2/3 in the plane. Under the remaining domain hypotheses, harmonic measures on balls and stopped walks also converge, with walks considered modulo time parametrization.

### Sphere Packings and Tangency-Polytope Tilings

For every nonempty open Ω ⊂ ℝ<sup>d</sup>, d ∈ {2, 3, 4}, there is a packing by arbitrarily small balls whose tangency polytopes form a locally finite, face-to-face tiling of exactly Ω. Ball tangencies correspond precisely to shared facets. Each tile has at most 6, 12, or 24 facets in dimensions 2, 3, or 4, respectively.

### Quantum Cones in Odd Dimensions

For odd d ≥ 3, 0 &lt; γ &lt; √(2d), and β &lt; Q = d/γ + γ/2, recentering the spherical-average process at its first crossing of −b after subtracting (Q − β)t gives a total-variation limit on each fixed radial tail as b → ∞. ### Reflected Random Walks with Spatial Singularities

Under the stated connectivity, mass-transport, ergodicity, and finite-moment assumptions on a planar cell environment, the interpolated reflected walk from a fixed starting cell, with area-over-conductance holding times, converges diffusively for almost every environment to Brownian motion with deterministic positive-definite covariance. The main note permits a closed singular set of zero one-dimensional Hausdorff measure and requires spatial local finiteness off that set. The companion treats whole-plane coverings with connectivity along almost every horizontal and vertical line, without that local-finiteness requirement.

- [Reflected Random Walks with Spatial Singularities](papers/reflected-random-walks.pdf) (40 pages)
