## Papers

PDF links open GitHub’s document preview. The collection currently contains 20 PDFs in 15 topics.

### The 3/8 Exponent for Shortest Directed Paths

The median n^(3/8+o(1)) scale for the maximum finite shortest directed-path distance in an unconditioned KMSW cell.

- [The 3/8 Exponent for Shortest Directed Paths](papers/sdp-three-eighths-exponent.pdf) (14 pages)

### Graph Limits of Longest Directed Paths

Tightness and continuous graph-valued subsequential limits of the rescaled longest directed-path field.

- [Graph Limits of Longest Directed Paths](papers/ldp-graph-limits.pdf) (15 pages)

### Nontrivial Limits of Shortest Directed Paths

Noncollapse of graph-valued, median-normalized limits, with a companion note on graph tightness.

- [Nontrivial Limits of Shortest Directed Paths](papers/sdp-nontrivial-limits.pdf) (7 pages)
- [Graph limits](papers/sdp-graph-limits.pdf) (22 pages)

### Mesh-Only Convergence for Orthogonal Tilings

Dirichlet approximation, harmonic measure, and walk convergence up to time parameterization. A recovered supplement gives harmonic-extension error of order mesh^(4/(d+4)), including the planar exponent 2/3.

- [Mesh-Only Convergence for Orthogonal Tilings](papers/mesh-only-orthogonal-tilings.pdf) (17 pages)
- [Sharper Mesh-Only Convergence Rates](papers/mesh-only-sharper-rate.pdf) (3 pages)

## Versions and scope

The selected source PDFs are preserved without changing their mathematical content. The three-page sharper mesh-rate supplement is a typeset transcription recovered from the original conversation and includes the original argument as an embedded text attachment. It should be read with the full mesh-only note.

The geodesic deviation and tightness notes do not identify a unique limiting law. The higher-dimensional LFPP exponent note identifies an exponent, not a leading coefficient.
